# OND product artwork — Field Note 05

Selected on 2026-11-17 from the 2026 OND reference sheet, Courtenay master display sheet and Eagle Creek OND sheet. Product SKU/format and source photographs matched against the connected Cascadia Shopify catalogue. The product manifest is public/ond-products.json. It intentionally excludes inventory and prices.

All selections except Catena are confirmed in the Courtenay display sheet. Catena SKU 478727 is on Eagle Creek display W15. Copper Moon SKU 881490 is a 4L bag-in-box, not the 750mL variant. There was no second 750mL Malbec confirmed in the checked lists, so the visual explicitly preserves the box format. Zaccagnini Pinot Grigio was excluded because its catalogue image depicts Pecorino; KRIS and Oyster Bay are used instead.

Eight transparent paired images were created with built-in image generation using the downloaded product photographs as references, then encoded as WebP with alpha preserved. They are realistic illustrations, not exact label facsimiles; use the manifest and current source product pages for identification, format and current details. No tasting claims were inferred from generated labels.

Output directory: public/art/ond/. Eight category images are used in the animated shelf and larger category selection view. Generation prompts follow.
